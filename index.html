02The CPO program

What "Certified Pre-Owned" means on a Subaru.

The term "CPO" gets thrown around loosely in used-car ads, so here's what applies specifically to the cars on our lot. Subaru of America's program covers vehicles within manufacturer-set model-year and mileage limits, and each one has to pass Subaru's 152-point inspection before it can wear the CPO badge.

The headline benefit is the warranty: Subaru CPO adds a 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty measured from the vehicle's original in-service date, on top of any remaining factory bumper-to-bumper coverage. You can confirm the current program terms directly on Subaru of America's own site, subaru.com. We'd rather you check the manufacturer's published terms than take our word for it.

Where the warranty clock starts

Most second-hand buyers miss this: that powertrain clock starts at the original in-service date, not the date you buy. So a CPO Outback that was first sold three years ago has already used three years of that 7-year window. It's still real coverage, but if you're buying a CPO car near the older edge of the eligibility range, do the subtraction before you assume you're getting the full seven years. Our finance desk will print the original in-service date for any CPO unit so you can see exactly where the clock stands.

What sends a car to the used line instead

When we take a Subaru in on trade, our techs put it on the rack and inspect the underbody, brakes, tires, and the boxer engine's gaskets and timing components before we decide whether it's headed for the CPO line or the standard used line. We've declined to certify cars over things a quick walkaround would never catch: a weeping head gasket, brake rotors past minimum thickness, tires that wouldn't survive a San Diego summer of freeway miles. Those cars still sell, but they sell as honest used units at honest used prices, not as CPO.

05In the service drive

What we look for.

San Diego is a coastal market, which changes what matters on a used inspection. We aren't fighting Midwest road salt and rusted-out subframes here. The bigger second-hand concerns in Southern California are different, and they're the systems our techs check first on any trade-in:

InteriorSun damage

Sun-baked interiors

The coastal-climate tell. Interior surfaces go brittle and crack after years of Southern California sun. We check them before any car goes on the line.

SealsInspected

Brittle weatherstripping

Heat-hardened seals. Door and window weatherstripping that's dried out in the heat lets in noise and water; we look at it on every trade-in.

A/CHard life

A/C systems

Summer-traffic wear. Air-conditioning that has lived a hard life in summer traffic gets checked, because it's a system that bites Southern California owners.

CoolingFreeway miles

Cooling systems

The hot-freeway risk. Cooling systems on cars that have done long, hot freeway commutes are exactly what fails on a hot day on the 805, so they come apart first.

We pulled the service history on a 2019 Forester trade-in and found two skipped coolant services. The car ran fine, but we flushed the system and replaced the thermostat before it went on the line. That's the work that never shows up in a listing photo.

That's why we'd tell you to ask any second-hand seller, here or anywhere, for the maintenance records before you sign. If a used Subaru you're looking at elsewhere doesn't come with service history and the seller can't explain the gaps, that's a reason to slow down, not a reason to panic, but a reason to budget for an immediate fluid-and-belts service. We'll happily run a pre-purchase inspection on a car you're considering, even one you didn't find on our lot. Reach the team through the official Dalton Subaru Contact Portal to set it up.

Lean used if

You're buying near the bottom of the price range, you plan to keep the car a long time, and you're comfortable budgeting for maintenance yourself. A clean, well-documented used Subaru with full service records can be the smarter buy than a CPO car whose warranty is already half spent.

Lean CPO if

You want the security of factory-backed powertrain coverage, you're financing and want predictable costs, or this is your first time owning a higher-mileage vehicle and the peace of mind is worth the premium.

CPO is not automatically the better deal, and we won't pretend it is. Bring us both options and we'll show you the actual numbers side by side.

Our advice: on a newer car with most of its original warranty intact, CPO adds real value. On an older eligible car where most of the 7-year powertrain window has already elapsed, a well-maintained standard used unit at a lower price can come out ahead.

Frequently asked questions about buying a Subaru second hand.

Where can I find a Subaru for sale second hand in National City?

What's the difference between a used and a Certified Pre-Owned Subaru?

A used Subaru is any pre-owned model on the lot. A Subaru CPO vehicle has passed Subaru's 152-point inspection, meets the brand's age and mileage standards, and includes a manufacturer-backed 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty measured from the original in-service date.

CPO costs a bit more and adds factory protection. Current program terms are published on subaru.com.

Does the CPO warranty start when I buy the car?

No, and this trips up a lot of buyers. The 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain coverage is measured from the vehicle's original in-service date, not your purchase date. On an older CPO car, part of that window may already be used.

We'll print the original in-service date for any CPO unit so you can see exactly where the clock stands.

Should I worry about rust on a used Subaru in San Diego?

Far less than a buyer in a salt-belt state would. The bigger second-hand concerns in our coastal, dry climate are sun-damaged interiors and weatherstripping, A/C wear, and cooling systems on high-freeway-mileage cars.

Our techs inspect those systems first on every trade-in, and we'd tell you to ask for maintenance records anywhere you shop.

Can you inspect a used Subaru I'm considering from another seller?

Yes. Our service team will run a pre-purchase inspection on a Subaru you're looking at elsewhere. It's the single best money you can spend before buying any second-hand car.
